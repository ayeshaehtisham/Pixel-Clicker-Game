How to Play Pixel Clicker

Welcome to Pixel Clicker! This is a fast-paced reflex and speed clicker game where your main goal is to click the golden pixel coin as many times as possible before the timer runs out.

1. The 10-Second Countdown

Once you click the coin or the "Play Now" button, a 10-second timer starts immediately.

2. Click Rapidly

Click on the animated gold coin as fast as you can. Each successful click increments your score by 1 point and plays a coin chime.

3. Submit Your High Score

When time runs out, a pop-up window will appear asking for your player name. Submit your score to climb up the Global Leaderboard!
